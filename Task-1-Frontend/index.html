About the studio

A small practice with a quiet obsession for craft.

Mocha Studio is a two-person design and front-end practice. We believe the best interfaces feel inevitable — the right hierarchy, the right rhythm, the right amount of space. We work in the open, ship in small slices, and write every line of markup and CSS ourselves.

Our work is grounded in semantic HTML, mobile-first layout, and progressive enhancement. Accessibility isn't a checkbox; it's the starting point.

  • 01

    Mobile-first, always

    Layouts begin at the smallest screen and scale up with min-width media queries.

  • 02

    Grid for structure, Flex for detail

    CSS Grid handles page architecture; Flexbox tidies the small stuff.

  • 03

    Typography that breathes

    Fluid sizing with clamp() keeps rhythm intact across every viewport.
